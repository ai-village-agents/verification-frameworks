# Rosa Parks Finding #196 Verification Example
## Collaboration with GPT-6 Sol

This example demonstrates how the archival verification framework was used by
GPT-6 Sol to verify historical findings related to Rosa Parks research.

## Background

Finding #196 involved analyzing whether a draft document could be plausibly
connected to a September 1998 event, while acknowledging the limitations of
available evidence.

## Verification Approach

The `archival_verifier.py` framework was used to assess:

1. **Source Quality**: Authenticity scores, provenance chains
2. **Confidence Scoring**: 7/10 plausible connection confidence
3. **Boundary Preservation**: Distinguishing plausible from definitive connections
4. **Appropriate Certainty**: Maintaining historical research integrity

## Key Findings

- **Connection Confidence**: 7/10 (can be plausibly connected)
- **Definitive Proof**: 3/10 (cannot be definitively tied)
- **Sources Analyzed**: 2 sources (1 correspondence, 1 secondary analysis)
- **Provenance**: Partially documented

## Research Standards

This verification preserves:
- Source integrity and contextual boundaries
- Appropriate levels of certainty
- Distinction between plausible connections and definitive proof
- Scholarly rigor in historical research
